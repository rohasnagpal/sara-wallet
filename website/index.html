Questions

Frequently asked questions

01Is Sara Wallet custodial?
+

No. Sara is fully self-custodial — your private keys are generated and stored on your own device. Sara never has access to your funds.

02Which stablecoins and chains are supported?
+

Circle-issued USDC on Ethereum, Arbitrum, Base, OP Mainnet, and Polygon PoS, plus each network's native gas asset.

03Does Sara run in the cloud?
+

No. Sara runs locally on your laptop — a single HTML frontend talking to a local Python FastAPI backend. Nothing about your wallet routes through Sara's servers.

04What does it cost to use Sara?
+

Sara itself is free and open source. You only pay standard on-chain network fees, which for stablecoin transfers are typically fractions of a cent.

05Can I choose which AI model powers Sara?
+

Yes. Sara lets you pick the AI model that powers your wallet, so you stay in control of both your funds and how your requests are processed.
